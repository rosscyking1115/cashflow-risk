# Product brief — one page

**Cashflow Risk Intelligence** — for UK small businesses that invoice on payment
terms.

## The problem

Late payments cost UK SMEs an estimated £11bn a year. A B2B service business or
freelancer sends invoices on 14–60 day terms, and a handful of customers carry
most of the revenue. When one of them slips, the shortfall lands with no warning —
most owners discover it when the balance is already too low to cover payroll, rent,
or a VAT bill. Existing tools (Float, Futrli, Fathom) forecast cash, but they are
accountant-facing, assume everyone pays on time, and stop at a chart — they don't
tell the owner *which customer to chase this week*.

## Who it's for

The beachhead: **UK B2B service businesses and freelancers with invoice payment
terms** — the segment that feels late-payment pain most and is least served by
accountant-centric tools. Accountants are a later, secondary channel.

## What it does

From a CSV of invoices, it produces three things:

1. A **13-week cash runway** forecast, timed by when invoices will *actually* be
   paid (not their due date).
2. A **ranking of invoices and customers by cash at risk** — outstanding amount
   weighted by the probability of late payment — each with a plain-English reason.
3. A **weekly action brief**: the chase list, the runway impact, and whether a
   shortfall is coming.

Every score has a "why"; every warning has a next step. It works from a CSV in
minutes, with a public demo to try before signing in.

## Why it's different (the wedge)

Not "forecasting exists" — that market is crowded. The wedge is **explainable,
action-first late-payment risk**: it turns a forecast into a decision. Customer
risk is enriched with Companies House signals (overdue filings, insolvency) for
incorporated customers.

## Framing

Decision support, **not** regulated advice — never tax, credit, or investment
advice. First-party data only; companies are profiled via Companies House, never
sole-trader individuals.

## What Phase 0 is testing

Before building further we need to hear, from real owners, that this changes a
decision they'd actually make. The validation question: *"would this output
change a chasing decision you'd make this week?"* See
[interview-guide.md](interview-guide.md).
