# Paper mock-up — the action brief

Show this to interviewees on a phone or a single printed page. It is the concrete
output of the product, in the exact format the engine produces (see
`scripts/demo.py`). Use the **shortfall** version as the primary prompt — it is
where the product earns its keep — and keep the second, calmer version to show the
"all clear" case.

Numbers below are an illustrative worked example for a fictional design studio, not
a real business.

---

## Northgate Design Ltd — as of 14 Jul 2026

18 customers · 31 open invoices · runway **7 / 13 weeks**

### ACTION BRIEF
------------------------------------------------------------
**Cash is projected to fall below your £8,000 reserve in week 7 (week of 25 Aug).**
The gap is driven by two large invoices from one customer that is trending late.

**Top cash-at-risk invoices:**
- **INV-2043: £14,200 at risk (61% late)** — Customer has paid late 74% of the time
  (11 prior invoices); customer's accounts are overdue at Companies House
- **INV-2039: £9,800 at risk (61% late)** — same customer; £24k outstanding across
  both invoices
- **INV-2051: £3,100 at risk (28% late)** — Long payment terms (60 days)

**What to do this week:**
- Chase **INV-2043 and INV-2039 now** — together they are the difference between
  clearing and breaching your reserve in week 7.
- Consider a payment plan or deposit for this customer's next job; their filings
  and payment history both point to distress.

*This is a cashflow risk estimate for planning support only, based on your own
data. It is not accounting, tax, legal, credit, or investment advice — review with
your accountant before acting.*
