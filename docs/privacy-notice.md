# Privacy Notice — draft

> Draft template. Fill the `[brackets]` and have it reviewed before publishing.
> Plain-English, for the app/landing page.

**Who we are.** [Legal entity], [address]. Contact: [email]. ICO registration: [ref].

**What this tool does.** You upload your invoice data; we estimate which invoices
are at risk of late payment and forecast your cash for 13 weeks, to help you
decide what to chase. It's **planning support, not accounting, tax, legal, credit,
or investment advice.**

**What we collect.**
- Your account details (name, email) via our sign-in provider, Clerk.
- The invoice data you upload — amounts, dates, and the customer names on your
  invoices — and your payment history with those customers.
- Results we generate from it (risk scores, forecasts, action briefs).

**What we don't do.** We discard your uploaded files after reading them (we keep
only the results). We don't sell your data, and we don't make automated credit
decisions about anyone. Risk estimates are **based on your own payment history**
with each customer — not on external credit data about individuals.

**Why we can process it.** To provide the service to you (our contract), and our
legitimate interest in computing the risk you asked us to.

**Who we share it with.** Our service providers only: Clerk (sign-in) and Render
(hosting and database), under data-processing agreements. [Hosting region:
UK/EEA — or state transfer safeguards.]

**How long we keep it.** Your results are kept until you delete them or close your
account, and analysis results older than 24 months are deleted automatically. You
can export or delete your data at any time.

**Your rights.** You can access, correct, export, delete, or object to our use of
your data, and complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk). Export ("Export my data") and
deletion ("Delete my data") are self-serve in the dashboard and take effect
immediately; for anything else — or if you can't sign in — email [contact] and
we'll respond within one calendar month (UK GDPR subject-access timescale).

**A note on customer data.** If your customers include sole traders (individuals),
their name and your payment history with them are personal data. We only use your
first-party history with them, never external profiling, and we don't share it.

**Changes.** We'll update this notice and the date below when things change.
Last updated: [date].
